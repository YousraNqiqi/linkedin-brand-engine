# Quick guide

## What this tool does
It turns a few details about your business into a LinkedIn marketing package: your ideal customer, market research, a stronger profile, a 30-day content plan, and ready-to-review posts. **It never posts for you.** You read, edit and publish everything yourself.

## Your first run in 3 steps
1. **Tab 1: Your business.** Describe what you sell, pick the closest style preset, paste your current LinkedIn headline and About text, and choose your AI provider and add your API key (or leave it empty to try demo mode).
2. **Tab 2: Strategy.** Press *Build my strategy*, wait a few minutes, then read every result.
3. **Tab 3: Daily post.** Tick the approval box, type a topic and press *Write today's post*. Edit the result and publish it by hand.

## Demo mode vs real mode
With no API key, the tool shows sample text so you can learn the screens. With your key, real AI writes for you and you pay your AI provider directly for usage. The tool works with a Claude key (console.anthropic.com) or an OpenAI key (platform.openai.com). Pick the matching provider on tab 1, set a monthly spending limit if offered, and never share the key.

## Before you approve, check
- Every number and claim is true, or deleted.
- Every [ADD REAL EXAMPLE] placeholder is replaced with a real story.
- It sounds like you when read aloud.
- No client is named without permission.

## Safe-use rules
- Publish by hand. Do not use this tool for automated posting, scraping or bulk messages.
- You are responsible for everything you publish.
- The AI works from general knowledge, not live web search, so verify topical claims.
- Your inputs and key are not saved after you close the page. Download your results to keep a copy.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Key rejected":** create a new key and paste it again with no spaces.
- **"No credit":** add credit with your AI provider.
- **Generic results:** add more detail about your offer, audience and voice, then rerun.
- **Slow or timed out:** untick steps you do not need on tab 1.
